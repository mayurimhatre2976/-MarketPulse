Comprehensive Retail Loyalty Capabilities

Engineered specifically for supermarkets, fashion stores, department outlets, and retail chains.

Automated Points Engine

Configurable backend conversion rules (e.g. 10 points per ₹100 spent) that automatically calculate and credit customer points on recorded sales.

Rewards & Voucher Catalog

Manage retail rewards with inventory stock thresholds, required points, expiry dates, and instant unique voucher codes.

Smart Coupon Management

Issue fixed and percentage coupons with minimum spend requirements, total usage caps, and active date range verification.

Promotional Campaigns

Broadcast seasonal festival sales, double-points weekends, and category discounts targeted directly to loyalty members.

Purchase & Invoicing Ledger

Maintain an accurate audit trail of customer purchase invoices, discounts applied, and resulting loyalty point earnings.

Secure Role-Based Portals

Dedicated interfaces for Store Administrators to manage inventory & sales, and Customers to track their personal points ledger.
